# Cedar Bridge: service outage and unavailable director

Fictional player briefing. Read before the decision round. All players can see this document.

## Situation

Cedar Bridge Community Services is a fictional organization that coordinates community appointments. Anika Rao is executive director. Morgan Ellis is service manager. IT support is an external supplier and cannot authorize board spending.

Appointment software is unavailable and IT has reported suspicious account activity. No data disclosure has been confirmed. The executive director is unavailable to coordinate this incident, and the continuity plan names no alternate incident coordinator. The board must decide how Cedar Bridge responds.

## Roles

1. CEO: leads the board discussion and interim coordination question, and chooses among tied voting leaders.
2. IT recovery lead: explains what IT must verify before anyone promises a restoration time.
3. Communications lead: separates confirmed facts from unknowns and proposes the next public update.
4. Service operations lead: represents the people who still need appointments and the approved paper process.

For three players, combine service operations with communications. Keep exactly one CEO. A role does not receive extra votes or private facts.

## Game rules

1. There is one decision round. Everyone receives the same public documents.
2. The round includes reading, timed speaking, discussion, a 60 second vote, and a result.
3. You may select a prepared response or propose a custom action during discussion.
4. Each player has one equal vote. The CEO chooses only if the leading options are tied.
5. The result describes narrative consequences. A later AI evaluation uses all committed answers and discussion; there are no fixed numerical gameplay meters.
6. Unknown information stays unknown. Requests outside the public documents receive "That information is not available in this exercise."

The participant pack's own fixture voting rules do not apply in this game; the live game rules above govern the vote.
