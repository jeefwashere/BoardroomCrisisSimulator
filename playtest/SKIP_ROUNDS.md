# Section controls with required decisions

Current X23 behavior supersedes the earlier X22 whole round skips. Read [the current guide](RECORDED_LIVE_AND_SECTIONS.md). Open [the updated app](http://127.0.0.1:8791/roundtable).

Select the CEO to skip the opening introduction, finish discussion early, or continue from completed expert perspectives to individual decisions. These controls sit below the meeting clock. They advance sections within Round 1, without skipping anyone's decision.

Private coaching answers, response card confirmation, assessment answers and reunion reviews cannot be bypassed. Stop agent speech or a Live session whenever needed, then give or review the required answer. There are no longer buttons that skip Round 1, skip preparation, skip assessment or finish the reunion before its required reviews.

Ordinary scoring and winner rules remain in effect. Historical incomplete runs created with X22 remain labelled incomplete. New requests for the removed bypass actions receive a conflict response from the server. The earlier [X22 integration record](evaluations/skip-rounds.json) is historical evidence of the superseded behavior, not the current controls.
