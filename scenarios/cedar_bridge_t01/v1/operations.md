# Continuity plan, version 1

Fictional public excerpt from the T01 participant pack. Available to every player before the decision round.

1. [BCP-1] The executive director is the incident coordinator. No alternate incident coordinator is named in this plan.
2. [BCP-2] The board may appoint an interim incident coordinator using the exercise voting procedure.
3. [BCP-3] The service manager can operate an approved paper appointment process if the board authorizes its activation.
4. [BCP-4] IT support must verify backup usability, recovery dependencies and a restoration estimate before the organization promises a service restoration time.
5. [BCP-5] The communications lead must distinguish confirmed facts, unknowns and the time of the next public update. No alternate communications channel is documented.

These clauses are part of the fictional case. The facilitator should not quietly add resources, staff, capacity figures or restoration times that the plan does not state.
