# Initial evidence: 14:00

Fictional public evidence. Available to every player before round 1. Later evidence arrives through the round updates.

## Security report

1. Three finance laptops display ransom notes and cannot open their local files.
2. Monitoring reports an unusual export attempt by `finance_sync`, an account used by the finance reporting bridge.
3. The bridge can read customer names, email addresses, order IDs, and delivery addresses.
4. Payment card details are handled by a separate payment processor. The team has no evidence that full card numbers are stored in this bridge.
5. The security team has not completed its investigation. A working storefront is not proof that the rest of the environment is unaffected.

## Customer support report

Two staff members say their finance tools have stopped working. Customer checkout still passes the routine availability check. No customer data complaint has been verified yet.

## Marketing message

The promotion is scheduled for 15:00. Marketing asks the leadership team to decide whether it should prepare a delay notice. It needs an answer soon enough to update customers consistently.

## Questions to investigate during discussion

1. Which facts are confirmed, and which are assumptions?
2. Which access can be restricted without automatically stopping every service?
3. Who owns the incident, and who collects the evidence needed for the next decision?

Players discuss these questions. They do not need to run commands or investigate a real computer.
