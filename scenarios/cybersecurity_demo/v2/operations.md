# Service and recovery notes

Fictional public operating assumptions. Available to every player before round 1.

1. The storefront and payment processor are separate from the finance reporting bridge. This makes targeted containment a plausible option in this fictional environment, not a guarantee for real systems.
2. The team can suspend the finance bridge and its account while monitoring the storefront. Doing so delays finance reconciliation.
3. Security logs are retained separately. The incident team can preserve available evidence while restricting affected access.
4. An isolated backup exists from 13:00. Its integrity and the recovery environment must be checked before it is used.
5. A controlled restoration is estimated to take 45 simulated minutes after validation begins. The promotion deadline may need to move.
6. Customer support can accept a limited queue of up to 200 orders through a previously approved manual process. This reduces capacity and does not replace full checkout.
7. Communications can publish a factual status update and commit to the next update in 30 simulated minutes. The update must distinguish confirmed impact from an investigation in progress.
8. The privacy lead and the incident team assess any notification duties for the relevant jurisdiction. This fictional exercise sets no real legal reporting deadline.
9. A ransom payment is outside this short scenario's decision set. The team is evaluating containment, communication, and recovery planning.

These assumptions are part of the fictional case. The facilitator should not quietly add resources or capabilities that contradict them.
