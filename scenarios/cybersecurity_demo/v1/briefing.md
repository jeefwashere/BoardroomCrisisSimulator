# MapleCart: the sale starts soon

Fictional player briefing. Read before round 1. All players can see this document.

You are the incident leadership team at MapleCart, a fictional online retailer with 80 employees and 25,000 customer accounts. A major promotion begins at 15:00. Thousands of customers are expected to visit the store.

At 14:00, three finance laptops display a ransom demand. At almost the same time, monitoring flags an unusual customer data export by the finance integration account. The storefront is still serving customers. Nobody has yet established whether the production environment is affected.

Your job is to protect customers, contain the incident, and make a credible continuity and recovery plan. Keeping the store open is valuable, but it is not the only objective.

## Roles

1. CEO: balances the final business decision and chooses among tied voting leaders.
2. Security lead: evaluates containment, evidence, and uncertainty.
3. Technology lead: explains service dependencies and recovery feasibility.
4. Finance lead: identifies operational costs and business tradeoffs.
5. Operations lead: proposes ways to continue essential work with limited systems.
6. Communications and privacy lead: separates confirmed facts from assumptions and coordinates accurate updates.

For three players, combine CEO with finance, security with technology, and operations with communications and privacy. For four or five players, combine responsibilities as needed while keeping exactly one CEO. A role does not receive extra votes or private facts.

## Game rules

1. There are three decision rounds. Everyone receives the same new evidence at the start of each round.
2. Each round includes reading, timed speaking, a 60 second vote, and a result.
3. You may select a prepared action or propose a custom action during discussion.
4. Each player has one equal vote. The CEO chooses only if the leading options are tied.
5. The story clock can jump forward between rounds. A decision to begin a 45 minute recovery does not mean recovery finishes during a five minute round.
6. Service continuity, data protection, and public trust are fictional game meters. They are not measured security effectiveness or probabilities.

The base schedule is about 18 minutes including briefing and debrief. Three to six players are recommended for this short version.
